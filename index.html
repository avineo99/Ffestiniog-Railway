Ffestiniog
Railway
Society

The Ffestiniog Railway Society exists to support the oldest independent railway in the world (being founded by an Act of Parliament in 1832) – now a historic and world heritage tourist attraction in North Wales.

The Society was founded in 1954 and for over 70 years has supported the Ffestiniog Railway through Fundraising, encouraging volunteering and supporting the unique heritage of the Ffestiniog Railway. Our work continues and we need support of our Members to ensure we are able to pass this much loved unique steam railway onto the next generation.
